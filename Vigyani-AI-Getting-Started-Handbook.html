Welcome to Vigyani AI

Vigyani AI helps you build an AI assistant ("agent") that talks to your customers on website chat, voice, phone and WhatsApp, in many languages. This guide walks you through it step by step. You don't need any technical background.

What is Vigyani AI?

Think of it as hiring a tireless front-desk assistant who never sleeps and speaks your customers' language.

Answers questions

It learns from the documents you upload (price lists, FAQs, policies) and answers from them.

Talks everywhere

The same agent can work in website chat, web voice, a phone line and WhatsApp.

Gets things done

It can collect details, take bookings, send emails and payment links, and create quotations.

Shows you results

See how many conversations it handled, what customers ask for, and how much time it saved you.

1Create your account

About 5 minutes, plus a short wait for approval.

  1. Open www.vigyani.ai and click Sign up.
  2. Choose Continue with Google, or Sign up with email and fill in your name, email and a password.
  3. If you used email, check your inbox for a "Verify email" message from Vigyani AI and click the link. (Check spam if you don't see it within a few minutes.)
  4. You'll see an Awaiting Approval screen. That's normal: a Vigyani administrator checks each new account. You'll get an email once your account is activated.
  5. After approval, go to Sign in and log in. You land on your Dashboard.
TipUse the same sign-in method every time. If you signed up with Google, keep using Continue with Google.
Seeing "Payment required"?Your account has an unpaid invoice. If you're the account admin, click View invoices to pay. Otherwise ask your admin.

3Add your AI keys

Your agent's "brain" and "voice" come from AI companies such as Google Gemini or OpenAI. You connect your own account with them using a key, a long password the provider gives you.

Why your own key?You only pay those companies for what your agent uses, and your data stays under your account. Vigyani has no shared key, so an agent can't run without one.
  1. Get a key from the provider's website, for example a Google Gemini key from Google AI Studio. Copy it.
  2. In Vigyani, open Settings → Vigyani Credentials and expand Vendors.
  3. Click Add credential, choose the provider, paste the key, give it a name you'll recognise (optional), and save.
  4. Click Test next to the key to check it works.
If your agent will…You needExamples
Chat in textOne "thinking" (LLM) keyGoogle Gemini (the default), OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq
Talk by voice or phoneThe LLM key, plus a listening key (speech-to-text) and a speaking key (text-to-speech)Listening: Deepgram or OpenAI. Speaking: Cartesia, ElevenLabs or OpenAI
Only admins see this page.If you can't find Vigyani Credentials, ask your account admin to add the keys, or to give you the Admin role.

4Teach it your documents

Upload what your agent should know: FAQs, brochures, price lists, policies.

  1. Go to Smart Sections → Vigyani Library.
  2. Click New folder and give it a clear name, such as "Hotel FAQs". One folder per topic keeps things tidy.
  3. Open the folder and click Upload Files. Accepted files: PDF, Word (DOC/DOCX), Excel (XLS/XLSX), CSV, TXT, Markdown, HTML, XML, JSON.
  4. Wait for the file to be read. A small icon next to the name means it's still working; hover over it to see the status. When the icon goes away, the file is ready.
You seeMeaningWhat to do
Indexing in progressThe file is still being read.Wait a minute or two. Big files take longer.
No iconReady to use.Nothing to do.
Indexing failedSomething went wrong while reading it.Delete it and upload again. If it fails again, contact support.
Indexing rejectedThe file type isn't supported, or the file is damaged or empty.Save it as PDF or DOCX and upload that.
ImportantUploading isn't enough on its own. In the next step you'll point your agent at this folder, so it knows to use it.

5Build your first agent

You build agents on a "canvas": a drawing board where you drag boxes and connect them. The easiest way to start is a ready-made template.

Start from a template (recommended)

  1. Go to Smart Sections → Vigyani Flow and click Create Workflow.
  2. On the left panel, open Templates and search for your kind of business: Healthcare Receptionist, Customer Support Center, E-Commerce Order Assistant, Restaurant Reservation Agent, Hotel Concierge, Travel Concierge and more.
  3. Click a template. It fills the canvas with a working agent you can change.

Make it yours

Use the three tabs on the left panel:

Identity

Give the agent a name (required). Optionally add an icon and a background picture.

Config

Choose where it works (Web Chat, Web Voice, Phone, WhatsApp), write its Greeting (the first thing it says), pick its languages and voice, and set Knowledge Scope to your Library folder.

Nodes

The building blocks. Drag one onto the canvas and connect it with a line.

Click any box on the canvas to edit it. The most important field is the agent's instructions: plain sentences telling it who it is and how to behave, for example "You are the friendly receptionist of Sunrise Clinic. Help callers book appointments. Be brief and polite."

The building blocks in plain words

Save

Click Create (or Save when editing). You'll see "Workflow saved… You can now publish it."

"Missing API keys"?The agent needs an AI key you haven't added yet. Click Add keys and follow step 3.

6Test & publish

"Publishing" switches your agent on. Always test it before sharing it with customers.

  1. On the canvas, click Publish (for voice agents: Publish & start voice) and confirm. The badge changes from Not Published to Published v1. Every new publish goes up a number (v2, v3…).
  2. Click Test. A new tab opens: type a message for a chat agent, or press Start and speak for a voice agent.
  3. Ask it the questions your customers usually ask. If an answer is wrong, improve the instructions or the documents, then Save and Publish again.

Share it with customers

  1. In Vigyani Flow, turn on the Chat switch on your agent's row.
  2. Open the ⋯ menu → Publish public URL…. Type a short name for the link (for example sunrise-clinic) or leave it empty for an automatic one, then click Publish public URL.
  3. Click Copy to put it on your website, social media or in messages. The ⋯ menu also has Show QR code, which is handy for posters and reception desks.
  4. Click Preview as visitor to see what customers see. Visitors sign in with Google before chatting.
Need to pause it?Use Stop Agent, or Unpublish the public link. Your link name is kept, so you can switch it back on later.

7Phone & WhatsApp

Go to Settings → Channels (admins only). Make sure the agent's Config tab has the matching channel ticked.

WhatsApp

1. Choose which agent should reply.
2. Click Generate code (or Generate QR).
3. On your phone: WhatsApp → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a device, then enter the code or scan the QR.
4. The status changes to Connected. Use Unpair to disconnect.

Phone line

You need a phone line from a telephone provider (called a "SIP trunk"). Save its login under Vigyani Credentials → Credentials, then click Register phone number. Enter the number with country code (e.g. +91…), choose incoming and/or outgoing calls, and pick the agent that answers.

  2. Click Invite member, enter their email, choose a role and click Send invite.
  3. They get an email and must accept within 7 days. Until then you'll see them under Pending invites.

Number of conversations, how fast the agent replies, and how each agent performs.

How much the agent used the AI services, which is what your provider bills you for.

Problems & answers

I signed up but I'm stuck on "Awaiting Approval".

New accounts are checked by a Vigyani administrator. You'll get an email when you're activated. If it's been more than a working day, contact Vigyani support.

I didn't get the verification email.

Check your spam or promotions folder for a message from "Vigyani AI". Still nothing? Try signing in again; there's an option to resend the email.

Saving or publishing says "Missing API keys" / "Add your provider API key".

Your agent needs an AI key that hasn't been added yet. See step 3. Voice agents need three keys: thinking, listening and speaking.

The agent doesn't know the answer, even though it's in my document.

Check that (1) the file has finished reading in the Library, (2) the agent's Knowledge Scope (Config tab) points to that folder, and (3) you published again after changing it.

I changed my agent but customers still see the old one.

Changes go live only after you Save and then Publish again. Check that the version number went up.

I can't see Credentials, Channels or Members.

Those pages are for Admins. Ask your account admin to do the step, or to change your role.

The agent quoted a wrong price.

Add or fix the price under Pricing Rules. The agent always takes prices from there.
